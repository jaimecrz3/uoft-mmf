## Course Overview

This course introduces the structure and functioning of modern financial
systems, with an emphasis on the relationships between financial
institutions, markets, monetary policy, and economic activity.

Topics covered include:

- The interconnectedness of financial institutions and the broader economy
- Commercial banks and financial intermediation
- Central banks and money creation
- Bank assets, liabilities, funding, and lending
- Financial markets, instruments, and market makers
- Information asymmetry and financial regulation
- Asset-price bubbles
- Monetary policy
- Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) and stablecoins
- Current challenges facing financial systems

The course places particular emphasis on understanding how different parts
of the financial system interact rather than studying them in isolation.

## Final Project

### Money Creation in the Modern Economy

**Type:** Team Project  
**Format:** Written report and presentation

The final project examined how money is created in modern financial systems,
based primarily on the Bank of England article *Money Creation in the Modern
Economy* by Michael McLeay, Amar Radia, and Ryland Thomas.

The project focuses on a central idea that is often misunderstood:
commercial banks create most broad money through lending.

When a bank grants a loan, it simultaneously creates a matching bank deposit.
Conversely, when a loan is repaid, the corresponding deposit is removed from
the banking system, reducing broad money.

The analysis also examines the role of central-bank reserves, monetary
policy, and quantitative easing.

### Topics Covered

- Broad money vs. base money
- How commercial banks create deposits through lending
- How loan repayment destroys money
- The limitations of the traditional money-multiplier framework
- Bank balance sheets
- Liquidity, credit risk, capital, and regulation
- The role of interest rates in constraining money creation
- Quantitative easing and portfolio rebalancing
- The relationship between reserves and bank lending
- Cryptocurrencies and stablecoins within the modern monetary system
