# Dynamic Data Science — MMF 1941

**Master of Mathematical Finance — University of Toronto**

This course focuses on applied financial data analysis and quantitative
research using real-world financial datasets.

The course follows a research-oriented approach built around data curation,
hypothesis formulation, empirical analysis, reproducibility, and clear
communication of quantitative results.

## Projects

### 1. Daily OHLC Spread Estimators

**Individual Research Project**

An empirical comparison of three spread estimators — Corwin–Schultz,
Abdi–Ranaldo, and EDGE — against realized intraday transaction-cost
benchmarks constructed from one-minute financial data.

The analysis investigates estimator accuracy across asset classes,
liquidity conditions, and market structures.

**Topics:** Market microstructure, transaction costs, liquidity,
high-frequency data, spread estimation

[View repository →](https://github.com/jaimecrz3/daily-ohlc-spread-estimators)

### 2. Price Pressure at the Close

**Individual Research Project**

An empirical study of whether large price movements into the closing print
reverse during the following overnight session, and whether closing-auction
activity helps absorb temporary price pressure.

The project examines the interaction between closing returns, auction
volume, and subsequent price reversal.

**Topics:** Market microstructure, closing auctions, price pressure,
overnight returns, liquidity
