# Risk Management - MMF2000

**Master of Mathematical Finance — University of Toronto**  
**Fall 2026**

## Course Overview

This course introduces contemporary risk management practices used by
Canadian banks and global financial institutions.

The course combines industry-standard terminology, quantitative methods,
and practical risk-management applications across several areas of financial risk.

Topics include:

- Regulatory capital
- Market risk
- Counterparty credit risk
- Treasury risk management
- Climate risk
- Liquidity risk
- Anti-Money Laundering (AML) risk
- Retail credit risk
- Stress testing
- Financial-crisis case studies

## Assignments

### Assignment 1 — Market and Counterparty Credit Risk

This assignment combines market-risk modelling with counterparty credit-risk
analysis using quantitative methods implemented in Python.

Topics covered:

- Black–Scholes full revaluation
- Delta and Delta–Gamma approximations
- Approximation error under large market shocks
- P&L attribution
- Greeks-based P&L decomposition
- VaR backtesting as a hypothesis test
- Breach probabilities and backtest power
- Counterparty exposure simulation
- Expected Exposure (EE)
- Potential Future Exposure (PFE)
- Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA)

### Assignment 2 — Regulatory Capital and Climate Risk

This assignment examines bank balance-sheet dynamics, regulatory capital, and
the incorporation of climate-related credit-risk adjustments into
risk-weighted assets.

Topics covered:

- Bank balance-sheet dynamics
- Equity-to-assets ratio
- Credit losses and their impact on bank capital
- Basel I risk weights
- Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA)
- Equity-to-RWA capital ratios
- Basel IRB credit-risk capital
- Probability of Default (PD)
- Conditional Probability of Default (CPD)
- Loss Given Default (LGD)
- Exposure at Default (EAD)
- OSFI climate scenario adjustments
- Climate-adjusted PD and LGD
- Impact of climate risk on RWA
